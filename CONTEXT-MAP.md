# Context Map

## Contexts

- [Todo Lists](./CONTEXT.md) — how people organize and track work in the todo application
- [Ideas and Results](./docs/ideas-and-results/CONTEXT.md) — how a foggy Idea becomes shared, pointable understanding. WIP vocabulary, not a skill; [handoff](./docs/ideas-and-results/handoff.md) for the next session.

## Relationships

- **Ideas and Results → Todo Lists**: Ideas and Results is how decisions about Todo Lists (and the system that implements them) are made precise enough to record. It does not own Todo, Todo List, or Next Due Date. Spec is one kind of Record, not the only one.
